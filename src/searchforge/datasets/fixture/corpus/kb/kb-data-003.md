# PII Handling and Anonymization

## Classification
Personal data such as names, email addresses and phone numbers is classified as PII and may only be stored in approved systems.

## Anonymization
Analytics datasets must replace direct identifiers with salted hashes, and aggregated reports must satisfy k-anonymity with k of at least 10.

## Access
Access to raw PII requires a documented purpose and is logged.
