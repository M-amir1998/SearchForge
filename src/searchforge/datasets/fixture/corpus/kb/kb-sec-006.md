# Data Retention and Deletion

## Retention periods
Application logs are kept for 30 days. Audit logs are kept for one year. Search query logs are aggregated daily and raw queries are deleted after 7 days.

## Deletion requests
When a customer asks for their personal data to be erased, the request must be completed within 30 days across all primary stores, caches, search indexes and backups as they expire.

## Exceptions
Data under legal hold is excluded from automatic deletion until the hold is released.
