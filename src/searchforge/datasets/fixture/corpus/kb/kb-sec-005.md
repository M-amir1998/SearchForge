# Security Incident Response

## Severity levels
SEV1 means confirmed unauthorized access to customer data. SEV2 means a credible threat or exposed credential without confirmed access. SEV3 covers policy violations without customer impact.

## First hour
Page the security on-call engineer, open an incident channel, preserve evidence and contain the threat. Do not delete logs or reimage machines before forensics approves.

## Disclosure
For SEV1 incidents affecting customer data, legal and the security lead decide on customer notification within 24 hours.
