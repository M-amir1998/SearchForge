# Quarterly Access Reviews

## Purpose
Access reviews enforce least privilege. Every quarter, managers confirm that each person in their team still needs the access groups they belong to.

## Process
The security team exports SSO group memberships on the first Monday of the quarter. Managers have ten business days to approve or remove each membership. Unreviewed memberships are removed automatically.

## Privileged access
Production administrator roles are reviewed monthly instead of quarterly, and every use of break-glass accounts is audited.
