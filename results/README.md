# Results

Evaluation and benchmark outputs. Everything here is **generated by SearchForge from measured runs**;
nothing is hand-written or estimated.

| Directory | What | Regenerate |
|---|---|---|
| `local-demo/` | **LOCAL DEMONSTRATION RESULTS** on the synthetic fixture collection (48 documents, 56 queries; core matrix B0–B5 plus fusion, candidate-depth and chunking experiments; adaptive-routing study). | `searchforge demo --out results/local-demo` and `searchforge demo adaptive --out results/local-demo` |
| `beir-scifact/` | First public-dataset study: BEIR SciFact test split (5,183 documents, 300 queries), CPU only. The dataset itself is downloaded to `data/` (git-ignored) and is never committed. | `searchforge datasets fetch-beir scifact` then `searchforge benchmark configs/benchmarks/scifact.yaml --out results/beir-scifact` |

Only small, curated summaries are committed (`report.md`, `metrics.json`, plots, gate/export JSON).
Per-run Parquet tables and per-run directories (`runs/`) are produced locally and git-ignored.

Fixture numbers demonstrate the methodology on a tiny synthetic collection. They are not
industry benchmarks, carry run-to-run latency noise on a shared developer machine, and
must not be quoted as evidence that one retrieval strategy is better than another in general.
